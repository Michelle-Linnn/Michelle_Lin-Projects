SPRAY CAN

Your phone is the can. The big screen is the wall.

  • 1Hold the phone like a TV remote: screen facing up, top edge pointing at the big screen.
  • 2Aim at the middle of the screen, then tap Start. That spot becomes the center.
  • 3Hold the big pad to spray. Pick a color on the phone, and shake the phone to switch brushes.
