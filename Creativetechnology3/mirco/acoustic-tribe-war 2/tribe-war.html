Sound check

Before the fight, teach the system who is who. Record the room, then each player alone. It learns a voiceprint for each of you, tries to pull your voices apart from a mixture, and scores how cleanly it can.

Input

Demo players are synthetic voices (Ember ≈ 112 Hz, Frost ≈ 228 Hz) so you can see the whole pipeline. Switch to Microphone to enroll real people.

Rules

The screen is a nutrient-rich chemical substrate u. Each player is a self-replicating chemical, vA or vB. Both eat the same substrate and poison each other where they meet. Voices that nobody enrolled grow a third, grey species.

∂vA/∂t = DA∇²vA + gA·u vA² − (F̄ + kA) vA − cA vA(vB + vX)
∂u/∂t = ∇²u − u(vA² + vB² + vX²) + F̄(1 − u)　F̄ = feed rate mixed by local concentration
  • Voice is the gene. Your enrolled pitch sets your species. A deep voice grows large, fast-spreading leopard spots; a high voice grows a dense labyrinth.
  • Volume is growth. The louder the tracker hears you, the faster you replicate and the more seeds land on your border.
  • A pitch jump is a mutation. A sudden change of timbre turns your pattern into travelling spirals for about 1.5 s and hits the opponent harder.
  • Room tone appears as one small glyph scattered across the field; its density follows the ambient level. Outsiders (voices that match neither voiceprint) seed the grey species at random spots.
  • Win: hold 70% of the field for 3 s. The winner floods the screen and the moment is kept as an acoustic fossil.
